# Responsible AI and scope limits

- Provenance: 5,000 fictional customers, synthetic transactions and probabilistic synthetic adoption outcomes. No real bank records, confidential data or London meter/customer identity linkage. All labels are simulations; performance is not real-world evidence.
- Leakage: disjoint customer partitions; pre-cutoff transaction features; asset/behaviour snapshots assumed pre-cutoff; preprocessing fitted only on training. Label fields and latent willingness are not predictors. Tests verify separation, future-transaction exclusion and saved imputer statistics. Merchant vocabulary is shared across synthetic partitions, making transaction evaluation optimistic.
- Reliability: propensity is an uncalibrated estimate under the synthetic generator. Confidence is null. Validation Brier/ECE and missing-feature counts are reported; no per-person confidence interval is claimed. Finance-product recall is poor at the documented 0.5 evaluation threshold.
- Fairness: income, age, employment and region can proxy protected or disadvantaged groups. No empirical fairness guarantee or real-population representativeness exists. Before deployment, evaluate subgroup calibration/error rates, data coverage and potential disparate impact with appropriate governance. Segmentation is exploratory, never a credit decision.
- Eligibility: screens check intended use cases only. Institutional product availability, affordability, credit, consent and suitability rules are not supplied and remain NOT_ASSESSED. A high model score cannot override exclusions. Missing ownership produces insufficient data; conflicts require review.
- Explanations: reason codes are deterministic evidence thresholds, not causal model attribution. The optional LLM selects only from backend-derived approved sentences. Exact validation rejects changed probabilities, impacts, eligibility claims, added attributes and unsupported advice. Timeout/malformed/refused output falls back. This restrictive scheme limits linguistic freedom; tests are guardrail tests, not proof that a free-form LLM is safe.
- Privacy: no keys in frontend; .env is ignored. Only structured fictional recommendation facts, without customer ID, are sent to OpenAI when enabled. Do not replace the demo dataset with real customer data without privacy review, lawful basis, access control, encryption, audit logging and retention controls. Provider data handling must be reviewed before production. The local app has no authentication and must not be publicly deployed with real data.
- Human oversight: every action is a demo explanation or a request to review evidence. No application, financial transaction, approval or investment advice is generated. Relationships/credit teams retain authority.
- Sustainability: documented UK annual operational factors, explicit scenario choices, physical units, nulls for missing information, no financed-emissions claim. Solar inputs are incomplete and paper lifecycle emissions are unavailable. Conditional opportunity totals are not achieved savings and must not be added across products.
- Evaluation: provider live availability is a separate dependency. A configured key did not yield usable output in the recorded smoke test; fallback worked. Offline validation results must not be reported as live LLM accuracy.
